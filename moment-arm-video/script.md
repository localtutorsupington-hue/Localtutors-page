# Script: "Why Do Some Lifts Feel Heavier?" (moment arm for kids)

- Length target: 60 to 90 seconds (about 180 words at a slow, warm pace).
- Reading level: third grade (short sentences, everyday words).
- Host: an original stick-figure host drawn in the same simple style as the reference images (same drawing in every scene; poses swap). The watermarked reference images are not used.
- Look: white background, black line art (matching the host), one accent colour for the distance line, one font throughout.
- Drawing rule for every exercise: a dot on the joint, a dashed line straight down from the weight (the weight's pull), and a thick coloured line from the joint across to that dashed line. That coloured line is the distance. It is clearly longer in "Harder" and shorter in "Easier."
- Captions match the voiceover word for word.

---

## Scene 1: Hook (about 8 s)
**Voiceover:** Hi friends! Why do some lifts feel heavier than others? Let's find out!
**Visuals:** Host slides in from the left in the thinking pose. On "Why" a small question mark pops above the host's head.
**On-screen text:** none (captions only)

## Scene 2: Backpack comparison (about 14 s)
**Voiceover:** Hold a heavy backpack close to your chest. Easy! Now hold it out with straight arms. Much heavier! The backpack did not change. The distance did.
**Visuals:** Two stick-figure panels side by side. Left, labelled **EASIER**: backpack hugged to the chest, short coloured line from the shoulder dot to the backpack's dashed pull line. Right, labelled **HARDER**: arms straight out, backpack far away, long coloured line. Host stands at the side and points at the long line.
**On-screen text:** EASIER / HARDER

## Scene 3: What is a moment arm? (about 15 s)
**Voiceover:** That distance is called the moment arm. It is how far a joint is from the line where a weight pulls. We will call it the distance from the joint to the weight's pull. Longer is harder. Shorter is easier.
**Visuals:** One arm close-up: a glowing dot on the elbow labelled "joint", a dashed line straight down from a dumbbell labelled "the weight's pull", and the coloured line between them labelled "distance". On "Longer is harder" the line stretches; on "Shorter is easier" it shrinks. Host gives a thumbs-up or point.
**On-screen text:** joint · the weight's pull · distance

## Scene 4: Biceps curl, elbow (about 14 s)
**Voiceover:** First, the biceps curl. The elbow is the joint. Arm hanging low? The weight is close. Easier. Arm sticking straight out? The weight is far. Harder!
**Visuals:** Side view, two panels. EASIER: forearm angled down (about 25 degrees from straight down), short coloured line from the elbow dot to the dumbbell's dashed pull line. HARDER: forearm level with the floor, coloured line as long as the forearm. Host appears with the tired reaction on "harder".
**On-screen text:** BICEPS CURL · EASIER / HARDER

## Scene 5: Side raise, shoulder (about 14 s)
**Voiceover:** Next, the side raise. The shoulder is the joint. Bent elbow? The weight stays close. Easier. Straight arm? The weight goes far. Harder!
**Visuals:** Front view, two panels. EASIER: upper arm out to the side, elbow bent so the forearm and dumbbell hang down under the elbow; short coloured line from the shoulder dot to the dashed pull line. HARDER: whole arm straight out to the side, dumbbell at the hand; long coloured line, the full arm length. Host points.
**On-screen text:** SIDE RAISE · EASIER / HARDER

## Scene 6: Squat, hip (about 14 s)
**Voiceover:** Last, the squat. The hip is the joint. Stand tall, and the weight stays close. Easier. Lean far forward, and your hips move far away. Harder for your hips!
**Visuals:** Side view, two panels, bar on the upper back with a dashed pull line straight down to the middle of the foot. EASIER: torso fairly upright, hip dot close to the dashed line, short coloured line. HARDER: torso leaning far forward, hips pushed back, long coloured line from the hip to the dashed line. Host does the thinking pose.
**On-screen text:** SQUAT · EASIER / HARDER

## Scene 7: Takeaway and call to action (about 10 s)
**Voiceover:** Remember: the longer the distance from the joint to the weight's pull, the harder it feels. Want more fun science? Learn with Local Tutors!
**Visuals:** Host centred and big, pointing. The rule appears in large text, then a simple end card with the brand name.
**On-screen text:** Longer distance = harder · Shorter distance = easier · Local Tutors
